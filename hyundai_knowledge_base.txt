1. HYUNDAI CRETA
=================================================================
Segment: Mid-size SUV
Price Range: Rs. 10.91 lakh - Rs. 20.11 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi)
Seating Capacity: 5 seater
Boot Space: 433 litres
Variants: 27 variants across trims (E, EX, S, SX, SX(O), Knight, King Edition etc.)

Engine Options:
- 1.5L Petrol (115 PS)
- 1.5L Turbo Petrol
- 1.5L Diesel (up to 157.57 bhp)
Transmissions: Manual, CVT, iMT, 7-speed DCT (Automatic)
Mileage (ARAI): 17.4 - 21.8 kmpl

Key Features:
- 6 airbags standard across all variants (including base)
- ABS with EBD
- Panoramic sunroof (top variants)
- Ventilated front seats (top variants)
- Hyundai SmartSense Level 2 ADAS: lane-keeping assist, forward collision
  avoidance, blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, smart cruise control
- Dual 10.25-inch screens (infotainment + digital cluster) on top-spec variants
- Electronic stability control, hill-start assist, tyre pressure monitoring,
  rear parking sensors

Recent Updates:
- Summer Edition launched at Rs. 12.06 lakh (adds features to lower/mid trims)
- SX Tech and SX(O) trims discontinued; line-up now has 7 trims
- Third-gen Creta (K3 platform) spotted testing, expected launch 2027

Competitors: Kia Seltos, Tata Sierra, Renault Duster, VW Taigun

13. GENERAL FAQs (for chatbot)
=================================================================

Q: Hyundai cars pe warranty kitni milti hai?
A: Standard warranty 3 years/unlimited km hoti hai, extended warranty options
bhi available hote hain up to 5-7 years (dealership se confirm karein).

Q: EMI kaise calculate hoti hai?
A: Example - Hyundai Creta ke liye EMI approx Rs. 24,619/month starts hoti hai
(60 months tenure, 9.8% interest, loan amount Rs. 11.64 lakh, down payment
Rs. 1.29 lakh). Exact EMI car model, loan amount aur bank ke hisaab se
alag hoti hai.

Q: Test drive kaise book karein?
A: Nearest Hyundai dealership visit karein ya official website
(hyundai.com/in) pe "Book a Test Drive" option use karein.

Q: Konse safety features standard hain?
A: Almost saare current-gen Hyundai models (Creta, Venue, Exter, i20, Verna)
me 6 airbags, ABS with EBD, aur electronic stability control standard hain.

Q: ADAS kya hai aur kaunse models me milta hai?
A: ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance System) Level 2 safety suite hai jisme
lane-keeping assist, forward collision warning, blind-spot monitoring jaise
features hote hain. Yeh Creta, Venue, Alcazar, aur Verna ke top variants
me available hai.

Q: Electric vehicle options kya hain?
A: Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, IONIQ 9, aur Creta Electric India me available
electric models hain.

Q: Finance/loan options kya hain?
A: Multiple banks (HDFC, Federal Bank, etc.) se car loan available hai,
typically 10-30% down payment ke saath.

Q: Creta aur Venue me kya difference hai?
A: Creta mid-size SUV hai (bada, zyada features, higher price range Rs. 10.91L-20.11L)
jabki Venue compact SUV hai (chhota, city-friendly, price range Rs. 7.99L-15.83L).
Creta zyada boot space (433L vs 375L) aur premium features deti hai.

Q: Sabse affordable Hyundai car kaunsi hai?
A: Grand i10 Nios sabse affordable hai, starting price Rs. 5.60 lakh (ex-showroom).

Q: Diesel variant kis model me milta hai?
A: Creta, Venue, Verna, aur Alcazar me diesel engine options available hain.
Chhoti cars (i20, Exter, Grand i10 Nios, Aura) sirf petrol me aati hain.

Q: Exchange bonus milta hai kya purani car pe?
A: Haan, purani car ke exchange par bonus milta hai jo model aur condition
ke hisaab se vary karta hai. Exact amount ke liye dealership se baat karein.

Q: Hyundai Creta Electric ki range kitni hai?
A: Battery pack ke hisaab se - 42kWh variant ~420km range deta hai aur
51.4kWh Long Range variant ~510km tak range deta hai (MIDC certified).

Q: Kaunsi car family ke liye best hai?
A: Agar budget Rs 15 lakh tak hai to Creta best family SUV hai (spacious,
safe, feature-rich). Bada family (6-7 seater) chahiye to Alcazar best hai.

Q: Bluelink kya hai?
A: Hyundai Bluelink ek connected car technology hai jo remote engine start,
vehicle tracking, geo-fencing, aur SOS emergency alerts jaise features deti hai.
Yeh higher trims me standard/optional milta hai.
